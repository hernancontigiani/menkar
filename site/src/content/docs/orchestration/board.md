## Open the Board

Click :ui[board.button] in the header, beside :ui[orch.switch]. It opens under the header, above
the tabs, and the same button closes it.

![](shot:board-default)

The columns are :ui[board.topics], :ui[board.queued], :ui[board.inProgress], :ui[board.waiting],
:ui[board.toReview] and :ui[board.done]. :ui[board.done] holds the tasks closed today; older ones
are folded at its foot. A dropped task is struck through.

## Act on a card

- Click a card to see its session.
- Right-click a task to :ui[work.star] or :ui[work.unstar].

Everything else is changed through the orchestrator.

## Resize the Board

The Board opens two cards high. Drag its lower edge, or choose :ui[board.showAll] or
:ui[board.defaultSize] in its bar. It reopens open or closed, and at the height you left it, after
a restart.
