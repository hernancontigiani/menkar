In the orchestration mode you talk to one session, the orchestrator. It hands each piece of work to
another session as a task, reads the reports, and brings you only what needs your answer: the
items under :ui[board.topics]. The sidebar shows those items and who does each task; the Board,
above the tabs, shows how far each task is.

![](shot:work-view-overview)

The mode is a setting of each project. Turn it off and the project shows the ordinary sidebar
again; turn it on later and the team is as you left it.

## Start a team

1. [Choose an orchestrator](/menkar/orchestration/set-up/) and turn the mode on.
2. Tell the orchestrator what you want done. Let it create the tasks and start the workers.
3. [Follow the work](/menkar/orchestration/follow-the-work/) in the sidebar, and
   [answer what awaits you](/menkar/orchestration/topics-and-tasks/) in the orchestrator's tab.
