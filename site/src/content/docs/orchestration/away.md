## Set yourself away

Click :ui[presence.setAway] at the foot of the sidebar, or press `Ctrl+B a`. Your state is
the whole app's, not a project's, and the control shows while some project has the orchestration
mode on.

An amber strip under the header of every window says since when you are away.

![](shot:away-strip)

While you are away, every orchestrator writes what happens into items instead of telling you in its
chat, goes on with what does not depend on you, and keeps new blocking items for your return. New
items make no sound, toast or desktop notice. The sessions' own desktop notifications still reach
you.

## Come back

Click :ui[presence.back] on the strip or at the foot of the sidebar, or press `Ctrl+B a`
again. Each orchestrator first answers with a short summary: how many things happened, and which
await your decision. Then go through them one by one, or ask for the full summary.

Quitting Menkar forgets that you were away: it always opens with you here.

## Answer when Menkar asks if you are back

Using Menkar does not bring you back by itself. A minute after you left, a key, a click or a scroll
turns the strip into a question with :ui[presence.notYet] and :ui[presence.back]. Choose
:ui[presence.notYet], or leave Menkar untouched for ten minutes, and it asks again only when you
come back after ten quiet minutes.

## Leave from your phone

When you follow the orchestrator through [Remote Control](/menkar/sessions/remote-control/), tell
it you are leaving and it sets you away. Writing to it while you are away does not bring you back:
it asks first, and brings you back only when you say yes.
